# Fix the mobile Ask Adrian keyboard experience

Opening Ask Adrian currently focuses the message field on every device. When a mobile browser shrinks or pans the visual viewport for the keyboard, the panel still fits the layout viewport and can lose its header or composer.

Keep the chibi launcher and the Obsidian & Arctic design. Use a compact bottom sheet on phones, including landscape, and a floating panel on tablets and desktops. Keep the header and composer visible while the middle scrolls.
