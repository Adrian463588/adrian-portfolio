# Content Guidelines — Anti-AI-Slop & Recruiter Clarity

1. **Direct, Evidence-First Copy:** State what was built or tested (`Cypress`, `Jenkins CI`, `AOL UniPaaS`, `Magic xpa`, `Kotlin`, `Tauri 2`) instead of generic claims (`seamless`, `leveraged`, `comprehensive`, `cutting-edge`, `robust`).
2. **Every Control Justified:** Do not place buttons, badges, or `Live Demo` links unless backed by a real destination (`https://...`). Internal employer engagements belong inside `Experience` as informational rows, not public clickable routes.
3. **Hero & About Conciseness:** Keep Hero focused on identity (`Adrian Syah Abidin` — `Software Engineer`), value proposition (`"I build and test software."`), and two clear CTAs (`View My Work`, `Get in Touch`). Keep About concise (two short paragraphs paired with custom avatar artwork).
4. **Verified Dates & Roles Only:** Preserve exact historical employment timelines (`Mar 2026 – Jul 2026`, `Jul 2026 – Oct 2026`), never claim completed roles as `Present`, and state confidentiality boundaries transparently where applicable.
